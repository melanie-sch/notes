When something is **accessible**, it can be obtained, used, and understood by everyone.

## Resources

- [Americans with Disabilities Act](https://www.ada.gov/topics/intro-to-ada/) covers accessibility laws in the United States

### Web accessibility

- [W3 Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/)
- [WCAG Quick Reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/) for viewing the guidelines at a glance
- [W3 Cognitive Accessibility](https://www.w3.org/WAI/cognitive/) is relevant to designing for [[neurodiversity]]
