**Neurodiversity** refers to the natural variations in how people think, learn, behave, and experience the world. It’s the idea that everyone’s brains work differently

## Notes on language

- **Neurodiversity:** The concept of neurological diversity.
- **Neurotypical:** A person whose brain functions in a way that fits within societal standards.
- **Neurodivergent:** A person whose brain functions differently from societal expectations.
- **Neurodiverse:** Describes a group of people that includes neurotypical *and* neurodivergent people.
