On new terms made up by our generation. “Quiet quitting” or “quiet firing”, for example.

All of these refer to things that have already been happening for a long time. We just didn’t have names for them. 

I think our generation/Gen Z is more vocal about calling these things out. I think we’re also more open to breaking down stigma and having these conversations on taboo subjects. So we/they came up with terms for them to continue having conversations about them. The Internet helps them spread more quickly, too.

One disadvantage is that we might assume a term refers to a specific phenomenon that is distinct from others. We might get lost in classifying what is happening, rather than paying attention how it affects us and what we can/should do about it. (“I’m sick of my job. But I don’t think it’s burnout.”)
