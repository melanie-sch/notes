Masking in social situations while living under surveillance.

Surveillance, in this case, meaning:

- Everyone has a camera in their pocket
- Technology is only getting more powerful and ubiquitous
- Videos of people’s behavior/existing in public go viral online
- People’s lives are ruined by viral videos/memes of them

As a result, people alter their behavior while in public. I think these effects of surveillance on the general population shares some similarities with how autistic folks mask. How might behavior modification under surveillance be similar/different to masking?

Panopticon effect
