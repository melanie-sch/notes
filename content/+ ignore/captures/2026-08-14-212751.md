On approachability in video games, particularly for those of all ages.

Thinking of the recipe book in Minecraft. Before it was added, you just had to know them. There was no way to learn that information in-game. I only learned those recipes from watching YouTubers play Minecraft.

Another thought: Pokémon type matchups. In newer games, you can see the effectiveness of your Pokémon’s moves on an opponent if you have encountered that species before.

It makes me wonder about the next generation of kids who are using the Internet for the first time. I imagine these features were added with them in mind.
